Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh from Our Ovens to Your Table

Good morning, neighbor! North Star Bakery is a small, family-run bakery where every loaf, pastry, and cake is shaped by hand and baked fresh each morning. Whether you are grabbing a croissant on your commute, picking up a loaf for family dinner, or planning a celebration, we are glad you are here.

A Welcome Message from Our Bakers

What We're Known For

  • Slow-fermented sourdough made with a starter we have fed every day for over ten years.
  • Buttery morning pastries like croissants, cinnamon rolls, and fruit danishes, ready by 6:30 a.m. for commuters.
  • Custom celebration cakes for birthdays, showers, weddings, and community events.
  • Local ingredients from farms and mills in our region whenever possible.
