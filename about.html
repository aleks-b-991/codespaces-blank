About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2014 in a tiny home kitchen, where our founder, Maria Lindqvist, baked bread for neighbors who kept coming back for more. Two years later, she opened our storefront on Maple Avenue with one oven, a sourdough starter named "Polaris," and a simple goal: to be a place the neighborhood could count on, like the North Star.

Today, our small team bakes hundreds of loaves and pastries every morning. We still shape every piece by hand, we still know many of our customers by name, and we still donate our unsold bread each evening to the Northfield Community Food Shelf.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with honest ingredients and strong local relationships.

  • Flour: Stone-milled wheat and rye from a family mill less than 60 miles away.
  • Dairy and eggs: Butter, milk, and eggs from nearby farms that treat their animals with care.
  • Seasonal fruit: Berries, apples, and rhubarb from local orchards and our Saturday farmers market.
  • No shortcuts: No artificial preservatives, and no frozen dough. Everything is made from scratch.
