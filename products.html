Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything on our shelves is made from scratch in small batches. Prices below are ranges because sizes and flavors change with the seasons. We do not take payment online. Simply send us a pre-order request and pay when you pick up.

Breads

Our breads are naturally leavened and slow-fermented for at least 18 hours, which gives them a crisp crust, an open crumb, and a rich flavor.
